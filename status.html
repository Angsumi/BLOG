Give Yourself a Question? “What is my status in your class or office”. When we say status in your class, immediately reminds you of marks you obtained in your last exam, and the rank you got with that mark. That rank can be taken as a status. In case of official rank like grade-1, grade-2, grade-3 job. These are some determinants of status in an office. But think! In an overall society, if someone ask, what is your status? (first of all no one ask like that). What will be your answer. If you consider the monthly income, then you can give yourself a rank.
